# What you can build

SnapServe runs the live phone conversation. Your product owns UI, leads, CRM, and business logic.

## Inbound voice assistants

Buy or bring a number, assign it to an agent, and answer customers 24/7 — appointments, support, FAQs — with tools that check calendars or create tickets.

## Outbound dialers & workflows

Trigger `POST /calls/outbound` from your backend when a lead fills a form, a deal moves stage, or a reminder is due. Get transcript + summary back via webhooks.

## Booking agents

Connect Google Calendar (or SnapServe scheduling), put booking tools on the agent, and let callers schedule themselves. See [Calendar & booking](/docs/calendar).

## Campaigns (dashboard)

Batch-dial CSV / Sheets / website / Meta leads with retries, dispositions, and lead transfer to webhook or Sheets. After-call WhatsApp/email rules live on the agent. See [Campaigns](/docs/campaigns).

## Meeting agents

Send the same agent into Google Meet, Zoom, or Teams — paste a link, auto-join calendar events, or dispatch from bookings. In-meeting tools cover leave, mute, chat, and slides. [Meetings guide](/docs/meetings).

## Apps on top of the API

- Custom CRM dialer UI
- Internal “call this contact” buttons
- Zapier-style automations / n8n workflows
- Post-call analytics from transcript + disposition
